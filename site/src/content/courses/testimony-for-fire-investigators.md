Confidence is not accuracy. Juries hear plenty of confident experts. What helps them is an investigator who can explain the method, admit its limits, and stay inside what the evidence supports.

## Who it's for

Investigators who testify in depositions, civil trials, or criminal court, and those who expect to start.

## What we cover

- Writing a report that matches your testimony
- Expert qualification and Daubert/Frye challenges
- Explaining fire science to people who aren't scientists
- Cross-examination: the questions you'll get about NFPA 921 and bias
- Saying "I don't know" and "undetermined" without apologizing for it
- Mock testimony with feedback

## Format

Short lectures and mock depositions. Every participant takes the stand at least once.

## Length

Two days (16 hours).
