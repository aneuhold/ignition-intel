## Who it's for

Investigators who testify, or will. Public and private sector.

## What we cover

- What Daubert and NFPA 921 expect from expert testimony
- Preparing your file before the subpoena shows up
- Explaining fire science to people who've never seen a fire scene
- Staying in your lane: answering the question that was asked
- Cross-examination: what to expect and how to stay calm

## Format

One day, including a mock direct and cross-examination for each participant.
