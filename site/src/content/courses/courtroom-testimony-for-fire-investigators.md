Overstated testimony has sent people to prison. Understated testimony lets real arson go uncharged. The fix for both is the same: stay in your lane, and say exactly what your data supports.

## Who it's for

Investigators who testify in criminal or civil cases, or expect to. Prosecutors and defense attorneys are welcome; it helps to hear how the other side thinks.

## What we cover

- Writing a report that shows your reasoning, not just your conclusion
- Qualifying as an expert: what the court is actually asking
- Daubert, Frye, and NFPA 921 as a standard of care
- Explaining "undetermined" to a jury without sounding unsure of your work
- Cross-examination: staying calm, staying accurate
- Moot court exercise with feedback

## Format

Two days, in person. Class size is capped at twelve so everyone gets time on the stand.
