## Who it's for

Investigators at every level, and evidence technicians. If you're working toward the IAAI Evidence Collection Technician (ECT) credential, this lines up well with it.

## What we cover

- Photographing a scene in a sequence that tells the story
- Sketching and diagramming: rough sketch to final diagram, with measurements
- Evidence collection, packaging, and chain of custody
- Drone imagery and Matterport 3-D scanning: what they add and what they don't replace
- Field notes that hold up when you read them three years later on the stand

## Format

Two days. Day two is in the field: you'll document a mock scene start to finish and we'll review it together.
