On every scene I've worked, my main focus was on documentation. The scene is gone the day you release it. What's left is your photos, your diagram, and your notes, and somebody may read them ten years from now.

## Who it's for

Investigators, evidence technicians, and fire marshal staff who photograph or diagram scenes. Helpful for anyone preparing for the IAAI Evidence Collection Technician (ECT) credential.

## What we cover

- A photo sequence that tells the story: exterior to interior, overall to close-up
- Scales, markers, and photo logs
- Scene diagrams by hand and by software
- Field notes that hold up years later
- Drone imagery and 3-D scanning: what they add and what they don't replace
- Evidence collection, packaging, and chain of custody

## Format

Classroom followed by a hands-on documentation exercise in a staged room.

## Length

Two days (16 hours).
