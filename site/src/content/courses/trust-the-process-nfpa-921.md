This isn't new. The scientific method has been the backbone of NFPA 921 for more than thirty years. What's still hard is using it at 2 a.m. in a wet basement with the owner asking when they can go back inside.

## Who it's for

Public and private investigators who already work scenes and want their process to hold up in peer review, in a deposition, and in their own head a year later. Useful for anyone preparing for IAAI-CFI or NAFI-CFEI.

## What we cover

- Recognizing the need and defining the problem
- Collecting data without deciding what it means yet
- Inductive and deductive reasoning, and where each belongs
- Testing hypotheses: cognitive testing, experiments, and peer review
- Negative corpus and why it doesn't meet the method
- Mapping your work to the NFPA 1033 job performance requirements

## Format

Two days. Case studies from my own files, with the parts I'd do differently left in.
