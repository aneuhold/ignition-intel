NFPA 921 isn't a checklist. It's a method for testing what you think happened against what the scene shows. This course walks through that method one step at a time, with real cases where it held up and a few where it didn't.

## Who it's for

Working investigators, public and private. If you've ever been asked on the stand "Did you follow NFPA 921?" and wanted a better answer than "yes," this is for you.

## What we cover

- The seven steps of the scientific method, applied to a fire scene
- Inductive and deductive reasoning, and where each one belongs
- Testing hypotheses: cognitive testing, experiments, and data from the literature
- Negative corpus, and why it is not a method
- When the answer is undetermined, and how to write it up
- NFPA 1033 job performance requirements and NFPA 1321

## Format

Lecture and case review. Bring a report you've written (names removed) if you want feedback on it.

## Length

Two days (16 hours).
