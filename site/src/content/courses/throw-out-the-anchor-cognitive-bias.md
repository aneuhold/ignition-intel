## Who it's for

Every investigator, at every level of experience. Experience doesn't protect you from bias. In some ways it makes you more confident in a first impression, and confidence is not accuracy.

## What we cover

- Anchoring, confirmation bias, and expectation bias, with examples from fire scenes
- Contextual information: what you should know before the scene, and what can wait
- Peer review that actually reviews something
- Documenting alternative hypotheses and why you rejected them
- Trust the process, not the feeling

## Format

Half lecture, half exercises. Some of the exercises are designed to catch you out. They caught me out too.

## Length

One day.
