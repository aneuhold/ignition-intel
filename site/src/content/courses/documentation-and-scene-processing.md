On my first scenes, my main focus was on documentation. It's still where I'd tell any new investigator to put their effort. Your report gets read long after the scene is gone.

## Who it's for

Investigators at any level, evidence technicians, and anyone working toward the IAAI Evidence Collection Technician (ECT) certification.

## What we cover

- Scene photography: sequence, coverage, and lighting in a burned structure
- Diagramming by hand and with software
- Drone overviews and Matterport 3-D scans: what they add and what they don't replace
- Evidence collection and packaging for ignitable liquid analysis
- Chain of custody and spoliation
- Field notes that turn into a clean report

## Format

Two days, in person. Day two is spent processing a staged scene in small teams.
