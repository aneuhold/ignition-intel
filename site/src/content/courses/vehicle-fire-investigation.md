Vehicle fires are some of the most common calls an investigator gets, and some of the least trained for. This course closes that gap.

## Who it's for

Investigators who already know structure fires and want to work vehicle cases with the same rigor. Useful preparation for the IAAI CFI-V endorsement.

## What we cover

- Vehicle fuel, electrical, and exhaust systems as ignition sources
- Fire spread in modern vehicles: plastics, composites, and battery packs
- Examining a burned vehicle methodically, exterior to interior
- Recalls, service history, and what the owner leaves out
- Incendiary vehicle fires and insurance fraud indicators

## Format

One day, in person, with hands-on examination of burned vehicles.
