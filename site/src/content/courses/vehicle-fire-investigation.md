## Who it's for

Investigators who already work structure fires and want to add vehicles, or who are working toward the IAAI CFI vehicle endorsement.

## What we cover

- Vehicle systems: fuel, electrical, exhaust, and hydraulic
- Common ignition sources and the evidence they leave
- Burn patterns on and in vehicles
- Documenting a vehicle at the scene and at the tow yard
- Recalls, service history, and other data outside the vehicle

## Format

Two days. Day two includes hands-on examination of burned vehicles.
