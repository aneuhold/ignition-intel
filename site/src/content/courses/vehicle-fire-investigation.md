## Who it's for

Investigators who handle vehicle fires as part of a mixed caseload, and anyone working toward the IAAI-CFI vehicle endorsement.

## What we cover

- Vehicle construction and the materials that burn
- Fuel, electrical, and exhaust systems as ignition sources
- Examining a burned vehicle step by step
- Recalls, service history, and what to request
- Incendiary vehicle fires and insurance fraud indicators, used carefully

## Format

One day. Includes a hands-on examination when a vehicle is available.
