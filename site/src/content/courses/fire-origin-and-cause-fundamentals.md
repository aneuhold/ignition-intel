This is the course I wish I'd had when I started. We work from the fire dynamics up, so you understand why a pattern looks the way it does before you try to interpret it.

## Who it's for

New investigators, fire officers moving into investigation, insurance adjusters, and anyone preparing for NFPA 1033 job performance requirements. No prior investigation experience needed. If you've never heard the term "origin and cause," that's fine: it means where the fire started and what started it.

## What we cover

- Fire dynamics you can use on scene: heat transfer, plumes, ventilation, flashover
- Reading fire patterns, and the ways they mislead
- Systematic scene examination, from the least damaged area to the most
- Developing and testing an origin hypothesis
- Cause classification: accidental, natural, incendiary, and undetermined
- Documentation that holds up: notes, sketches, and photographs

## Format

Three days, in person, with a burn-cell exercise on day three. Also available as a six-week online cohort with a recorded field walkthrough.
