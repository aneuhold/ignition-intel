## Who it's for

Firefighters, fire inspectors, and new investigators who are, or soon will be, responsible for origin and cause. No investigation experience required. If you've pulled ceiling during overhaul and wondered what you just destroyed, you're in the right room.

## What we cover

- Fire dynamics you actually use at a scene: heat transfer, plumes, ventilation, flashover
- Reading fire patterns, including the V-pattern, and what they can and can't tell you
- Working a scene from the least damaged area to the most damaged
- Arc mapping basics
- Building and testing hypotheses under NFPA 921
- When "undetermined" is the right answer, and how to write it

## Format

Three days. Classroom in the morning, scene exercises in the afternoon when the host agency can support a burn cell or acquired structure.

## What you leave with

A repeatable method, a scene notes template, and a reading list that goes deeper than the slides.
