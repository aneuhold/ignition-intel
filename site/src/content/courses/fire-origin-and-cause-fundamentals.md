## Who it's for

New investigators, firefighters moving into investigation, and insurance or legal professionals who need to read an origin and cause report with a critical eye. No prior investigation experience is required. If you know what overhaul is, you're ahead.

## What we cover

- Basic fire dynamics: heat transfer, fire growth, ventilation, and flashover
- Reading fire patterns, and what they can and can't tell you
- Working a scene from the least damage to the most damage
- Identifying the area of origin before you go looking for a cause
- Ignition sources, first fuel ignited, and the circumstances that brought them together
- Where NFPA 921 and NFPA 1033 fit into all of it

## Format

Classroom sessions with case studies from real scenes, followed by a practical day working a burn cell. You'll document it, diagram it, and present your findings to the group.

## Length

Three days. Can be delivered on site for agencies and departments.
