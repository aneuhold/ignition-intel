## Who it's for

New investigators, firefighters moving into an investigation role, and adjusters or attorneys who need to understand how an origin and cause opinion gets built. No prior investigation experience required. If you've worked overhaul, you already know more than you think.

## What we cover

- Fire dynamics in plain terms: heat transfer, fire growth, ventilation, and flashover
- Fire patterns and what they can and can't tell you
- Working a scene from the least damaged area to the most
- Arc mapping as a tool for narrowing the area of origin
- Identifying potential ignition sources and first fuels
- Why "undetermined" is a valid scientific answer

## Format

Two days, classroom plus a live burn cell walkthrough where available. Every topic ties back to NFPA 921 and the job performance requirements in NFPA 1033.

## What you leave with

A repeatable process for working a scene, a set of field worksheets, and a clear idea of what you need to document before anyone moves anything.
