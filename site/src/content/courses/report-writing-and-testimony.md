Confidence is not accuracy. A jury can't tell the difference, so it's on us to know where our expertise ends and to say so.

## Who it's for

Investigators who write origin and cause reports or expect to testify, and newer investigators who haven't been cross-examined yet.

## What we cover

- Report structure that follows the scientific method
- Saying "undetermined" clearly, without apologizing for it
- Language that overstates, and what to write instead
- Preparing for deposition and trial
- Staying inside your qualifications on the stand
- Mock direct and cross-examination with feedback

## Format

Two days. Day two is in a courtroom setting when one is available.
