## Who it's for

Investigators who write reports or expect to testify, in criminal or civil cases. Also useful for attorneys who want to understand how a fire investigation report is built.

## What we cover

- Structuring a report around the scientific method
- Stating your opinions, the basis for each, and their limits
- Expressing certainty honestly, including "undetermined"
- Depositions and trial testimony: preparation, direct, and cross-examination
- Common ways testimony overstates the evidence, and how to avoid them
- Stay in your lane: knowing where your expertise ends

## Format

Lecture, report reviews, and a mock testimony session with feedback.

## Length

Two days.
