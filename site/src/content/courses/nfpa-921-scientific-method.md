## Who it's for

Working investigators who know the steps on paper and want to apply them under real conditions: limited time, a collapsed structure, and people waiting on an answer.

## What we cover

- Recognizing the need and defining the problem
- Collecting data without deciding what it means yet
- Analyzing data: inductive reasoning and what counts as a pattern
- Developing hypotheses, plural
- Testing hypotheses with deductive reasoning, including cognitive testing
- Selecting a final hypothesis, or calling it undetermined

## Format

One day. Lecture in the morning, case work in the afternoon. You'll work two real scenes from the photos and notes, in small groups, and present your hypothesis testing to the room.

## Length

Eight hours. Eligible for IAAI and NAFI continuing education credit (confirm with your certifying body).
