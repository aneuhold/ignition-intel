Every one of us brings expectations to a scene. Mine included. This course is about building a process that catches them before they become conclusions.

## Who it's for

Working fire investigators, supervisors who review reports, and attorneys who need to understand how expectation bias, confirmation bias, and anchoring show up in fire cases.

## What we cover

- How expectation and confirmation bias work, with the research behind them
- Anchoring: why the first story you hear is so hard to let go of
- Throw out the anchor: structured techniques for testing alternate hypotheses
- Managing task-irrelevant information from police, owners, and witnesses
- Peer review that actually reviews
- Case studies where the first answer was wrong, and how the process caught it

## Format

One day, in person or live online. Works well as an add-on to an agency in-service training.
