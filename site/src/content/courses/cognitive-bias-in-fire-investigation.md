## Who it's for

Any investigator, public or private. Supervisors who review reports will get a lot out of it too.

## What we cover

- Anchoring, confirmation bias, and expectation bias, with fire-scene examples
- How early information (a witness, a detective, a client) shapes what we look for
- Negative corpus and why NFPA 921 warns against it
- Peer review that actually reviews
- Writing reports that show your work

## Format

Half day. Discussion-heavy. Bring a case where you changed your mind. I'll bring a few of mine.

## Length

Four hours.
