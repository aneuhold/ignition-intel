Bias is a feature of normal cognition, not a character flaw. Everyone in this profession has it. The question is whether your process catches it before it reaches the report.

## Who it's for

Any investigator, supervisor, or attorney who reads fire reports. Also a good fit for agencies building a peer review program.

## What we cover

- How the first piece of information you hear becomes an anchor
- Confirmation bias in scene examination, interviews, and lab requests
- Contextual information: what you need to know, and what you're better off not hearing yet
- Alternative hypotheses as a habit, not a formality
- Practical controls: checklists, blind review, and documenting what you ruled out

## Format

One day, or a four-hour block for conferences and in-service training.
