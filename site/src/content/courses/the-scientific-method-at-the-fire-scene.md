## Who it's for

Working investigators who know the steps of the scientific method and want to apply them under real conditions: limited time, a collapsed structure, and people waiting on an answer.

## What we cover

- Recognizing the need, defining the problem, and collecting data
- Inductive and deductive reasoning, and where each belongs
- Developing hypotheses that can actually be tested
- Testing by cognitive and experimental means
- Negative corpus, and why NFPA 921 calls it out
- When to call a fire undetermined, and how to write it

This isn't new. The method has been in NFPA 921 for decades. What's hard is following it when the scene, the clock, and your own experience are pushing you toward an answer.

## Format

Lecture and group case reviews. Bring a report you've written, if you can share it. We'll walk the method through it together.

## Length

Two days.
