On my first big scene as an investigator, my main focus was on documentation. It still is. The scene is gone the day you release it. Your documentation is what's left.

## Who it's for

Investigators, evidence technicians, and fire officers who document scenes before an investigator arrives. Supports the IAAI Evidence Collection Technician (ECT) objectives.

## What we cover

- Photography sequences that tell the story without you there to narrate
- Sketches and diagrams, by hand and in software
- Drone imagery and Matterport 3-D scans: what they add and what they don't replace
- Field notes that someone else can follow
- Evidence identification, collection, packaging, and chain of custody
- Spoliation, and how to notify interested parties

## Format

Two days, hands-on. Bring your camera.
