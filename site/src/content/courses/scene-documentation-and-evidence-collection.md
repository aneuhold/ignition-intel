## Who it's for

Investigators and evidence technicians who want a documentation habit that holds up in a report and on the stand. Useful preparation for the IAAI Evidence Collection Technician certification.

## What we cover

- A photography sequence that tells the story of the scene, outside to inside
- Sketches and diagrams that are measured, labeled, and readable
- Field notes you can still understand two years later
- Aerial documentation with a drone, and 3-D scanning with Matterport
- Identifying, collecting, packaging, and preserving fire debris and other evidence
- Chain of custody and spoliation

On my scenes, my main focus was on documentation. Everything else in the investigation depends on it.

## Format

Classroom instruction followed by a full documentation exercise at a prepared scene.

## Length

Two days.
