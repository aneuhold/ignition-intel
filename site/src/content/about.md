## Investigating

I'm a Principal Consultant at Envista Forensics, where I've worked since 2019, and I've done private fire investigations since 2014. I'm a licensed private investigator in Oregon, Washington, Alaska, and Montana, a licensed drone operator, and I document scenes with Matterport 3-D scans.

Over my career I've worked more than 3,500 fire and explosion investigations: residential, commercial, vehicle, and outdoor fires, including fires complicated by explosions, injuries, arson, and fatalities.

## Teaching

I've taught for as long as I've investigated. At Portland Fire & Rescue I was a fire training specialist, and later I trained the city's new fire investigators. In 2013 I became an instructor and partner at Arete Investigations. I'm an IAAI Certified Instructor and a NAFI Certified Fire Investigation Instructor.

I've seen good investigators reach the wrong answer, and I've been one of them. Usually the problem wasn't knowledge. It was skipping a step, trusting a first impression, or saying more on the stand than the evidence could carry.

My courses are built on NFPA 921, 1033, and 1321, and on the habits that keep an investigation honest: follow the method, document everything, watch for bias in yourself as well as others, and accept that "undetermined" is a scientific answer, not a failure.

## How I got here

The only police-like class I could get into in 1992 was Fire Investigation 101, and it got canceled. I signed up again the next term. (Until then, my biggest win was a coloring contest in the 80's.)

I started as a volunteer with Canby Fire District in 1996 and stayed 17 years as a firefighter, engineer, and lieutenant. In 1998 I joined Portland Fire & Rescue as a firefighter, then worked as a fire training specialist and a fire inspector. From 2007 to 2024 I was a sworn Fire/Arson Investigator and police officer there.
