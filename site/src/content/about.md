I'm Nicole Brewer. I've been in the fire service for 30 years, and I still work fire scenes: as a Principal Consultant at Envista Forensics since 2019, and on private fire investigations since 2014. I've been teaching investigators for most of that time, from training Portland's new fire investigators to teaching as an IAAI and NAFI certified instructor.

I joined Canby Fire District as a volunteer in 1996 and spent 17 years there as a firefighter, engineer, and lieutenant. I started with Portland Fire & Rescue in 1998 as a firefighter, then worked as a fire training specialist and a fire inspector. From 2007 to 2024 I was a sworn fire and arson investigator and police officer there.

Somewhere past 3,500 fire and explosion investigations, I noticed the hardest part of the work wasn't the fire. It was us. Our expectations, our first impressions, the story we heard on the way to the scene. Confidence is not accuracy. That idea runs through every course I teach.

I'm a licensed private investigator in Oregon, Washington, Alaska, and Montana, and I live in Molalla, Oregon.

For the record, I also won a coloring contest in the 80's. And the only police-like class I could get into in 1992 was Fire Investigation 101. It got canceled. I took the hint anyway.
