I'm Nicole Brewer. I spent 30 years in the fire service, 26 of them with Portland Fire & Rescue, and the last 17 of those as a sworn fire and arson investigator and police officer. I retired in 2024. Now I teach fire investigation to working investigators and to people just getting started.

I joined Canby Fire District as a volunteer in 1996 and spent 17 years there as a firefighter, engineer, and lieutenant. In 1998 I started with Portland Fire & Rescue: firefighter, fire training specialist, fire inspector, and from 2007 on, investigator. Part of that job was training Portland's new fire investigators.

Somewhere past 3,500 fire and explosion investigations, I noticed the hardest part of the work wasn't the fire. It was us. Our expectations, our first impressions, the story we heard on the way to the scene. Confidence is not accuracy. That's what I teach now.

Today I'm a Principal Consultant at Envista Forensics and a licensed private investigator in Oregon, Washington, Alaska, and Montana. I live in Molalla, Oregon.

For the record, I also won a coloring contest in the 80's. And the only police-like class I could get into in 1992 was Fire Investigation 101. It got canceled. I took the hint anyway.
